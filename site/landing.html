The safeguards that let you stop worrying about it

Test mode first

Until you switch it off, every customer email and text comes to you instead, labelled with who it was meant for. Your sheet is not touched.

Only the right people

Only invoices marked as open get reminders. Paid, paused, disputed or blank rows are skipped and listed in your summary so you can fix them.

Problems are loud

A bounced email or a disconnected account sends you a plain-English alert with a link to what went wrong. Nothing fails silently.

Your accounts, your data

Everything runs in your own n8n, Google and Stripe accounts. I never hold your passwords, and if we stop working together it all keeps running.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need to know anything about n8n?

No. n8n is the automation tool everything runs on. I create the account in your name with you, set it up, and show you the one place you might ever want to change wording.

What if a client has already paid?

Set the invoice to Paid in your sheet and reminders stop on the next run. Anything you are not sure about can be set to Paused.

Will an AI say something I didn't approve?

The reply is written only from facts you approve, and it is never allowed to quote prices, promise dates or offer discounts. If the AI is unavailable or unsure, a fixed reply you wrote goes out instead. You can also skip AI completely.

What does it cost to run each month?

An n8n plan or a small server, which you pay n8n or your host directly. Gmail and Sheets are usually already part of your setup. Text messages and AI replies cost a few cents each, and both are optional.

What happens if something breaks?

You get an email that says what stopped and why, usually "reconnect your Google account". Fixes are covered during your support period and on the care plan.

I use QuickBooks, Xero or a CRM. Does that work?

Yes, as an add-on. The standard setup uses a simple invoice sheet, and I can connect your accounting system or CRM instead.
